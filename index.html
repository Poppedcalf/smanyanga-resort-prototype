Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM onwards, and check-out is by 10:30 AM. Early check-ins or late check-outs can be requested subject to availability.

How do we get to Samanyanga Resort in Kariba?

You can reach Kariba via a scenic drive from Harare or via scheduled/charter flights into Kariba Airport. Airport shuttle and private transfers can be arranged directly through our concierge.

Are meals included in the room rates?

Most rates include a full gourmet breakfast. Half-board and full-board packages covering lunch and dinner at our main lakeview restaurant are also available during booking.

Is boat cruising and fishing booked on-site?

Yes, our front desk and activity desk can arrange sunset boat cruises, fishing equipment, boat hire, and guided safaris at your convenience.
